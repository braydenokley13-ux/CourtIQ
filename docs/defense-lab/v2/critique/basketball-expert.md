# CourtIQ adversarial basketball review (defense lab, HIGH P&R -> tag -> lift/skip)

Method: throwaway vitest runs against `simulate / analyze / explore / attack / saveToSystem` (files deleted, source untouched).
Default = `createDefaultConfig()` (drop, over, big 3.2 m, tag 0.95, x-out, on-pass, counter lift, seed 2026, reaction 0.30 s).
Coordinates: x across, z from attacked baseline, rim z=1.575. "lateBy" = `defenderNeeds - releaseIn`.

## Verdict in one paragraph
The thesis claim is real: changing the defense measurably changes where the offense goes (below). That is the genuine moat over FastDraw (static drawings) and VReps (film/recorded reps). But the numbers a coach sees are brittle, three headline UI claims are not what the sim did, the "Their offense" selector is mostly placebo, and "expert control" tops out at 2 hard-coded if/then sentences plus global sliders. A skeptic can currently win "untrustworthy simulator" in about five minutes.

## P0 (trust-breaking; fix before showing a coach)

**P0-1. Headline moment narrates a play the sim did not run.** Default moment = `drive/O3 deep-tag t=3.77, "lane open 1.0 s, late by 0.43 s"`. Copy: "The far-corner shooter drives past his man to the basket" / "Layup ready in 0.38 s". Reality (frames): O3 catches at 3.50 in the corner (-6.5,1.35), dribbles along the baseline 1.5 m, shoots at 4.47 from (-5.03,1.40), 5.0 m from the rim; nearest defender is 3.4 m away (D3), so there is no "man" to drive past. At the freeze D3 is already 4.4 m from the roller and recovering (v 2.8 m/s), so "your helper goes all the way to the screener" describes t=2.08, not the frozen frame. Cause: threat id `drive` is overloaded (handler pull-up, roller finish, any catcher's "keep"), and `finishReady` waits `driveAt+0.7 s`. Fix (engine): split `keep` into `spot-up` (shot at catch+gather, release point = catch point) vs `attack` (real dribble toward rim), and give ThreatOption a `finish: 'layup'|'jumper'` from the release distance. Fix (UI): drive `MomentNumbers` "Layup/Finish" label and `explainMoment` headline from that field, never from the threat id.

**P0-2. Defense abandons the closeout the instant the catcher "keeps" the ball.** `defenseResponsibilities` treats `passSeen` as `phase in {pass,gather,shot}`; after O3 catches and the read resolves to keep, `ball.phase='handle'`. Trace (default): 3.77-3.90 D4 `closeout:corner`, D3 `closeout:lift`; at 4.10 D4 flips to `split:lift+split:corner`, D3 flips to `tag:roll + recover:corner` (re-tags a roller who has stood at (0.75,2.07) since 2.8 s) with the corner shooter holding the ball. Result: D4->O3 distance goes 5.43 -> 4.93 -> 3.52, nobody closes. The "1.00 s open" window (and the +0.43 s lateBy) is inflated by this reset. Fix (engine): latch "ball has been passed this possession" (`runtime.passCount>0`) or `owner !== ballhandler` instead of the instantaneous phase; closeout stays until contest or shot.

**P0-3. Brittle verdicts presented as facts.** Same config, only seed changes (capability jitter 0.96-1.00 per player): over 60 seeds the default is `breaks drive/O3` 49x, `thin roll/O5` 5x, `breaks ...late-rotation` 6x. Switch: `holds` 36, `breaks` 12, `thin` 12 (so "Switch holds" is 60% a coin flip). Hedge moment is `lift/O4 deep-tag` 44/60 but `roll/O5 big-too-high` 8/60. Numerics: default at dt 0.0125 gives read chain roll@1.10>lift>corner>drive (cause late-rotation); dt 0.025 gives lift@2.08 (deep-tag); dt 0.05 gives `thin`. reactionDelay 0.15 flips breaks->thin; gatherTime 0.45 flips ->thin. Fix (engine): evaluate each verdict over an ensemble (e.g. 12 seeds x reaction +/-0.1 s) in the worker and show "held in 9 of 12" or a range for window/lateBy; pin dt as a non-user constant and add a convergence test (dt/2 must not change the read chain). Fix (UI): replace "holds/breaks" binary badge with "held 9/12".

**P0-4. "Their offense" counters are mostly cosmetic.** vs default drop, counters `auto, roll, lift, skip, extra` produce byte-identical runs (reads lift@2.08>corner@2.83>drive@3.77, same windows). Same for `auto/roll/lift` under hedge, blitz and ICE. "Roll: look inside first" visibly throws the lift pass. Cause: counter only adds a +0.22 read bonus (`readCandidates`), which never beats arrival-gap value. Only reject, pop, slip, short-roll change motion. A coach pressing "Skip -> one more" and seeing the same film as "Roll" will stop trusting the tab. Fix (engine): make counter a hard first-read constraint for `passCount===0` (force or forbid options), then report "forced roll pass: deflected/contested by D3" honestly; add a "no-read" mode so the coach can see the failed version of a counter. Fix (UI): grey out/annotate counters that were identical to Auto ("same as auto vs this defense").

**P0-5. Switch never creates the mismatch it is famous for.** Switch (auto): offense reads only `drive` (O1 keep at 2.25, shoots 2.95 from 3.2 m with D5 1.0 m away); `roll:gap -0.59`, no post/seal/lob to O5 (2.02 m) against D1 (1.87 m). D1 is assigned `towardRim(roller,0.85)`, i.e. a guard fronting a big between him and the rim, which is the best possible spot for the small. The only size effect is `contestScale` clamped to 0.85-1.2. Explore then recommends "Switch the screen" for the default (improves 5 windows, opens only drive/O1 0.30 s, class holds). For a tactician this is the most-dismissed claim: a switch that costs nothing. Fix (engine): size-aware read value (height delta feeds `value`, seal/lob flight), and screener-side offensive response to a switch (re-screen/slip/seal as `BehaviorRule`s with `deferReadFor`); switch responsibilities should target the screener's high side, not the rim side. Also exposes late switch / switch-1-through-4 as parameters.

## P1 (credibility / expert ceiling)

**P1-1. Defense-changes-offense: PROVEN, with gaps.** Offense-player divergence vs default drop (first >0.1 m, max gap): ICE 0.30 s / O1 5.17 m (handler rejects, reads roll first); switch 0.63 s / O1 3.8 m (reads drive only); blitz 0.93 s (roll>drive); tagDepth 0.2 1.45 s (lift never leaves: `lift-behind-tag` needs D3 vx>0.2 and x-gap>3.9 m, an emergent trigger that works); stay 3.15 s. This is the product's best evidence; surface it as a "what the offense did differently" diff, not just window deltas.

**P1-2. Offensive counters do not create new defensive responsibilities.** `attack()` on five defenses (drop, drop+stay, switch, tag off, hedge): new responsibilities under the selected attack = NONE in all five; dropped = NONE. `defenseResponsibilities` is a pure function of (answer, bodies); reject, re-screen, short-roll only move bodies. Real consequence missing: reject should flip who is low man/strong-side nail; short-roll should hand the tag to the weakside defender. Also `attack()` selected `base` (zero edits) in 3/5, because the default offense already breaks the defense, so "Break my defense" often returns "your current offense already breaks it" (26/26 candidates witnessed). Fix (engine): add `on-activation` assignments in `DefensiveBehaviorRule.when` (`{kind:'activated', ruleId}` already exists in the algebra) and ship defaults for reject/short-roll; make Break Mode rank by *minimal new defender obligations*.

**P1-3. Dead zones in sliders.** tagDepth 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 give identical reads (roll@2.25 > drive@3.25, shot O5@3.25) and near-identical windows; 0.6 -> 0.8 flips holds->breaks (lateBy 0.41 -> 0.32 but chain changes). `lift-rise` coach rule at the UI default (1.5 m) NEVER activates in the default scenario (lift reaches only +1.34 m by the pass at 2.0 s); at 0.5/1.0 it activates at t=2.0/2.2 s, after the first pass, so stay vs x-out change nothing. Checking the box creates a rule that cannot fire. Fix (UI): show "this rule fired at 2.2 s / never fired" beside each rule using `sourceRuleId` from frames (data already exists); clamp rule thresholds to the observed range.

**P1-4. "Late by" is a lower bound, labeled as measurement.** Moment copy says "Measured from this run". `defenderNeeds` is a straight-line, body-free estimate; realized first-defender-within-1.25 m after the freeze: default predicted 0.81 s vs realized 1.38 s; stay 1.55 vs 1.72; hedge 0.91 vs 1.33; blitz 0.79 vs 1.05. Estimator is optimistic for the defense by 0.2-0.6 s, so some "late by +0.16" moments might be fine in the replay. Fix (UI): label "best-case arrival", show the realized replay arrival beside it, and mark the moment `contradicted` when they disagree by >0.2 s.

**P1-5. Personnel is invisible to the coach.** Engine supports per-player `speed, acceleration, lateral, reach, height` (`ProblemPlayer`, `resolveCapability`). With `simulateProblem` on a cloned problem: D3 speed 0.85 -> lateBy 0.43 -> 0.56; D3 1.10 -> 0.25; D4 0.85 changes first read to lift@1.93; D3 1.80 m + O5 2.15 m flips the read chain (roll first) and cause to late-rotation. But `labConfigSchema` has no personnel field, `problemId` is `z.literal(HIGH_PNR_PROBLEM.id)`, and the UI "Top speed" is one global `maxSpeed` for all defenders (offense speeds are authored constants, so the opponent athlete cannot be changed at all). "Set them to your players" in the hint is not possible. Fix (engine+UI): `config.personnel: Partial<Record<PlayerId,{speed,accel,lateral,reach,height}>>` merged in `simulateProblem`; UI: five defender cards + opponent "athleticism" slider.

**P1-6. Saved Our System entries do not capture the coach's logic.** `saveToSystem` stores the full executable `config` (rules, interventions, opponent), good. But `versionDiff` compares answer keys only: changing opponent.rescreen, reactionDelay 0.4 and adding a timed switch cue yields `["Same rules (re-saved)"]`; coach-rule threshold 1.0 -> 2.5 yields `["coach rules changed"]` with no detail. `when` is a 120-char free string never evaluated; `resolveFor` matches `situationId+scope` only; scope `lineup` carries no lineup. So exceptions ("against their 5 star, switch") cannot be stored or resolved. Fix: structured `when` (condition ref from PolicyCondition), include interventions/opponent/assumptions in diff, lineup scope with personnel hash.

**P1-6b. ICE is weak.** ICE = POA target x-0.8 m (screener side) + same drop big. With reject off, ICE vs drop max player gap 2.2 m, reads roll>lift>corner vs lift>corner. With reject on (default) the offense simply rejects at 0.68 s; there is no sideline/baseline pressure, no big wall-off, no weakside shift ("Middle-screen approximation" disclaimer is honest but a tactician will call it a drop with a shaded guard). Add `force: 'sideline'|'baseline'|'middle'`, big `wall` position, and weakside "nail" shift.

**P1-7. Timeline looks slow for high-school execution claims.** Handler holds ~1.7 s after the screen (0.42 -> first pass 2.08); shot at 4.47 s (4.0 s after screen). Plausible for pick-and-pop trap breaks, long for "ready" HS reads; and `rotationTiming:'early'` assumes the backside anticipates at tagDepth>0.45 with a 0.30 s reaction (not an HS-trainable cue). Label as "modeled", keep.

## P2 (polish / terminology)
- `Skip` is assigned by pass length >6 m: lift (-5.1,7.5) -> corner (-6.5,1.4) is a 6.3 m weakside drop-off/baseline pass, not a skip; the first O1 -> O4 pass (the actual cross-court skip) is called "Pass". Name kinds by geometry (skip = strongside to weakside across the lane).
- "Offense reads -> keep" for a catch-and-shoot; call it "shoot / attack".
- `hedge` produces `5 takes pop` then `5 takes roll` within 0.05 s (0.80/0.85 s): visible churn in the event chain.
- Fix list ranks "rotate earlier" for a deep tag; result: drive/O3 1.00 -> 0 but drive/O4 0.30 -> 1.00 (lateBy 0.43 -> 0.62). Honest, but the card should say "moves the problem".
- Explore `cause: 'unknown'` for any expert movement cue (e.g. moving D4 to the lift until ball-leaves gives moment cause unknown, lateBy 0.71): cue-driven runs lose their explanation.
- No make/miss, turnover, deflection or foul. Honest ("no make/miss prediction") but a competitor demo will ask "so is 0.3 s worth it?". See ceiling item 10.

## Expert ceiling today
Expressible: coverage preset x 5, POA over/under, big depth 1.5-6 m, tag on/off + depth, backside x-out/stay, early/on-pass, recovery; 2 coach rules (roller-depth, lift-rise; <=1 each; disabled under switch); timed `answer`/`opponent` patches ("edit from this moment"); `move` cues (drag, until time or `ball-leaves`/`big-secured`); opponent screenAngle, liftDelay (liftWidth in engine only), reject/rescreen/shortRoll toggles; global reaction/speed/pass speed.
Artificial ceiling: one problem (`problemId` literal); roles fixed (who is low man cannot change); triggers are only time or two hard-coded observations although `PolicyCondition` already has distance/coordinate/relative/responsibility/possession/activated; no per-player numbers; offense is a perfect reader.

**10 most-wanted expert controls**
Existing primitives (UI/schema work only):
1. Per-defender personnel (speed, lateral, reach, height) -> `ProblemPlayer` fields exist.
2. General if/then builder over `PolicyCondition` -> `DefensiveBehaviorRule` exists (today 2 sentence templates).
3. Event-triggered interventions (`when: PolicyCondition` instead of `at: seconds`).
4. Role swaps (who is low man/nail/X) -> `roles` map exists, assignments are role-based.
5. Offense counter branches (reject -> re-screen -> short roll) authored by coach -> `BehaviorRule` graph with `excludes/activated/deferReadFor`.
6. Show-me-fired/never-fired per rule -> `sourceRuleId`/`policyActivations` already in frames.
7. Opponent athleticism and liftWidth in UI (`liftWidth` is in the engine/schema, absent from FullControl).
Needing new primitives:
8. Shot quality / shooter profile / make probability and turnover/deflection (reads currently use arrival gap only).
9. Force-direction and sideline geometry (true ICE, red/ice/weak), hedge show-height/duration, switch numbering, late switch.
10. More problems (side P&R, Spain, 5-out DHO) and a player-skill model for offense (pull-up threat that changes how high the big plays).

## Competitive critique
Beats FastDraw/Hudl/VReps/Second Spectrum-like tools: (a) a live, non-scripted opponent that reads the defense (reads differ by coverage: first read roll@1.10 vs lift@2.08 vs drive-only), (b) causal chain with the named defender and seconds, (c) executable fixes with cost ("closes drive/O3 1.00 s, opens drive/O5 0.30 s"), (d) honest scope statements (no make/miss), (e) saved executable answers rather than drawings.
Skeptic wins today: (1) "just FastDraw in 3D": five counters are identical films, so the offense looks scripted; (2) "scripted animator": reads are deterministic and one verdict flips with +/-4% jitter; (3) "VReps with graphics": no real film/opponent tendencies, no personnel, no shot values; (4) "untrustworthy": P0-1/2/3, estimator shown as measurement; (5) "a one-play demo": one problem is hard-wired in the saved schema. Second Spectrum-style tools win on real tendency data; CourtIQ's only defensible lane is "teach and test your rule against a non-cooperating opponent", so the opponent must visibly refuse to cooperate (P0-4, P0-5, P1-2).

## Trust: fake precision
- Windows and lateBy print 2 decimals (`0.43 s`, `+0.43 s`, `0.81 s`). Resolution is dt 0.025 s, reaction is a 0.3 s guess, the estimator is best-case, and seeds move lateBy by +/-0.05 and flip verdicts. Show 0.1 s (or a range) and an N-of-M ensemble.
- `holds` means "no window longer than gather+read (0.41 s)", not "stop": tag=false holds while O5 (2.02 m) catches at the rim and finishes uncontested, nearest defender D5 3.3 m away at the release (shot O5@3.25).
- classification "holds" coexists with a moment saying a defender is "late by 0.15 s" (switch default). Reconcile or hide the moment when the verdict is holds.
- The global "Top speed" slider affects defenders only (offense actions have authored speeds) yet sits under "Our players & assumptions" with "set them to your players".
