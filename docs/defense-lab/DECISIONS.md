# CourtIQ V1 implementation decisions

Updated 2026-10-05 for the founder’s Blender/world-engine checkpoint. The world-first redesign supersedes the first build's title band, reserved rules deck and result-modal composition.

## Product boundary

One complete defense-first problem: high P&R, low-man tag, weakside lift/skip and subsequent catch reads. No film, tracking, analyst, login or connected service is needed. Build, run, inspect, change, compare, attack, retest, save and teach are executable browser workflows.

The gym fills the workspace below a small masthead. Four experiment actions float over the court. Role selection produces an anchored coaching card; placement avoids projected athletes when possible. One X-ray layer is shown at a time. Break Mode auditions only completed tested routes, promotes the selected route, plays its actual possession, and freezes at the first executable witness. Fix attaches the role editor and defaults rule edits to a new starting answer; movement demonstrations still begin at the frozen clock. Fix status stays in the mode bar while the retest action stays in the experiment dock, avoiding a second card over the role editor; retesting preserves the previous opponent as evidence. Searching traces are alternate complete replays, not branches proven to share a state. Compare uses one court with synchronized prior ghosts, actual defensive trajectories and difference-only divergence vectors.

## Opposing systems

Serializable observation predicates, role-relative motions, latched transitions and priorities interpret content rules. Defense changes the opponent's observed opportunities. Reactive lift, reject, short-roll and bounded re-screen can chain into actual catch/pass/extra decisions. A separate baseline-drive fixture exercises generic observed triggers and defensive stunts without P&R clock-based switching.

Detailed P&R coverage/tag formulas remain authored domain policy. This is reusable runtime machinery plus one bounded corpus instance; it does not implement arbitrary philosophies or formation-independent low-man eligibility.

Initial re-screen permission is off. It remains available to Break My Defense, preserving a clear initial tag/lift problem without deleting escalation.

## Evidence and trust

Geometry, movement integration, windows and reproducible traces are computed. Thresholds, movement parameters, pass preferences and basketball rules are authored assumptions. Shared isThreatOpen drives numbers, visual colors and attack witnesses.

Pending read commitments are included in release horizons; an opening during a committed action is a forecast under current geometry. Attack witnesses additionally require actual executable permission: a real read with the candidate available, or an ongoing chosen drive. A forecast window that disappears before permission is not a break.

Arrival assumes an unobstructed route. Structural conflicts require a stronger two-deadline feasibility test. Pass and full shot arcs are tested against interpolated analytical capsules at probes no farther than 5 ms apart. Contact stops physical possession at the first witness; no downstream action is invented. Catch support shares size, facing, hands and jump geometry. The unresolved result does not count as a held possession. Unsupported legacy continuations retain conditional evidence.

No global defensive score, shooting odds or possession prediction is used. Finite search is neither an exhaustive tactical proof nor a game-outcome forecast.

## Causal coaching and teaching

A rule/movement edit begins at its timestamp and preserves earlier frames. Targets integrate through speed/acceleration limits; released balls never retarget. Stored-frame lookup tolerates decimal-tick rounding without showing the preceding witness frame.

Templates interpret only supported statements, preview every changed rule and reject incompatible switch/tag instructions. Two coach-owned conditional sentences compile through the same observed predicates and role obligations as content: roller depth and lift rise. They require an observed screen and handler possession, normalize precedence and suspend under switch. The displayed sentence previews the response; the coach never edits a graph. Actual obligations carry source-rule provenance, so role teaching distinguishes an additional read from the resumed base answer. Templates that replace help/rotation explicitly preview removing those additional reads; an on-ball cue preserves them. Nested rule snapshots are independent across frames, presentation, input and saved configuration. The tag rail evaluates the actual policy at zero/current/full commitment; dragging changes the portable rule while the defender executes through motion limits. Demonstrated floor targets can release on observed ball departure or roller security; current defensive rules resume afterward.

Strict versioned records contain configuration, opponent intent, assumptions, timed cues, terminology and teaching checkpoints. Device storage has memory-only fallback; export/import preserves inputs. Teach loads the exact saved snapshot, separates primary present job from conditional recovery, and restores the experimental draft on return.

Before/after attack comparisons preserve the previous answer's defender movement cues as well as its rules. Current opponent intent is shared across the pair; the coached geometry is never silently copied into both answers.

## Presentation and performance

A reproducible Blender/DCC pipeline builds anatomical athletes, a reusable basketball action library and high/tactical geometry LODs. The browser blends native animation clips on one shared skeleton palette per actor, with bounded wrist/sole presentation corrections. Original authored Blender equipment replaces the procedural hero hoop, bench boxes and padding. Sources, licensing, export/validation scripts and CPU render references are retained.

Normal and analytical worlds are separate representations of the same physical snapshots. The reversible wash replaces normal rigs with instanced articulated envelopes; porcelain is the default for body/readability continuity, while spectral remains a prototype reachable through ?xrayLook=spectral. Actual sampled future reach, flight segments, ownership and conflict geometry add evidence without choosing basketball actions. Analytical envelopes are authored physical approximations, not the detailed athlete animation mesh.

Simulation and rendering are decoupled. A worker performs bounded search; UI history is limited to three entries/800 frames. Frozen rendering is on demand; live rendering has a 30 Hz submission cap, adaptive resolution and athlete LOD, instanced analytical bodies and wall pads. CPU profiles target measured movement/avoidance hot loops; no speculative WASM/WebGPU dependency was added. Cloud speedups do not certify integrated-GPU laptop performance. See runtime-performance.md and runtime-performance-protocol.md.

The result is deliberately stylized real-time basketball. It remains below the founder reference’s photoreal rendering and is not measured biomechanics.
